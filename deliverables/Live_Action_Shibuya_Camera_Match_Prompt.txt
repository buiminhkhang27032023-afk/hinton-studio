Analyze the entire previs before composing the shot. Follow its actual sequence and timing rather than inventing a separate edit.

Match the subject's size and position within the frame at successive moments. Preserve changes in foreground/background parallax.
Camera translation is not digital zoom. A downward crane move physically changes height and occlusion. An orbit changes viewing azimuth and background alignment. A backward dolly increases camera distance. Use lens zoom only where the reference actually calls for that perspective behavior.
Keep architectural edges stable. No breathing buildings, elastic streets or background texture sliding independently of camera motion. Follow a clear route around physical obstacles. Never pass through a person, curb fixture, wall, tree, vehicle or building.
If the reference is one continuous take, retain one continuous take without hidden redesign. If it contains cuts, match their timing and view changes only. No extra drone opening, reaction close-up, reverse shot, hero pose or random dramatic camera movement.
Motion must remain smooth and cinematic without robotic stops, uncontrolled shake, speed ramps or artificial slow motion absent from the reference.
